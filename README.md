## 🧭 Overview

Friday night. Everyone's ready to go out, but nobody knows where. Groups end up bouncing between Instagram reels, WhatsApp groups, Google Maps, YouTube videos and restaurant apps for hours just to settle on one place.

**What Scene?** is a social planning app concept built around one idea: *the problem isn't a lack of options, it's too many options.* The app collects each squad member's vibe, budget, cuisine and distance preferences, then uses AI to produce a short, explainable list of venues the whole group can agree on.

![What Scene? hero](assets/hero.png)

## 😵 The Problem

**Planning paralysis is real.**

Typical flow today:

`Instagram → WhatsApp → Google Maps → Too many options → Indecision → ❌ Plan cancelled`

Core pain points identified:

| Pain point | Pain point |
|---|---|
| Too many choices | Endless scrolling |
| Fragmented discovery | Decision fatigue |
| Budget mismatch | Planning exhaustion |

## 📖 Where It Began

This project started from a personal experience: a group of friends spending hours switching between Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Maps, YouTube and Zomato just to pick one place to go.

> *The problem wasn't lack of options. It was too many options.*

---

## 💡 The Pitch

**AI handles the chaos.**

What Scene? helps friend groups decide where to go faster by giving simple AI-based recommendations instead of hours of searching across different apps, so users can focus on the vibe.

- ⚡ Faster group decisions
- 📱 Replaces 3+ apps
- 📍 One place for your squad

## 🔍 Competitor Analysis

**The gap nobody filled.**

| Platform | Solves | Misses |
|---|---|---|
| Zomato | Delivery & reviews | Group decision making |
| Google Maps | Navigation & places | Collaborative filtering |
| Instagram | Visual inspiration | Actionable planning |
| Swiggy Dineout | Table booking | Vibe-based discovery |

**Opportunity:** no existing product combines group preference-sync, vibe-based discovery and actionable planning in one flow.

## 📱 App Concept & Features

- **Plan Something:** one-tap entry to start a new plan from the home screen
- **Vibe-based discovery:** "What's the vibe today?" (rooftop, chai, cheap eats, desserts, cozy, late night…)
- **Squad invites:** invite via WhatsApp, Instagram, copy link or contacts; plans stay private to invited people
- **Preference sync:** each member picks mood, budget, cuisines (at least 3), travel distance and dietary preferences in about 10 seconds
- **AI recommendation:** combines everyone's inputs into a ranked shortlist, each with a *"Why it's the perfect match?"* explanation
- **Swipe to pick:** members swipe on venues (like / skip / save for later)
- **Scene locked:** final pick can be opened in Google Maps or shared with the squad
- **Explore & Saved:** browse by vibe, save places into collections and lists

## 🔄 Task Flow

| Step | Screen | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Home** | User opens the app |
| 02 | **Create Plan** | User taps "Plan something" and sets name, occasion, date, location and time |
| 03 | **Invite Squad** | User selects contacts or shares an invite link |
| 04 | **Squad Joining** | Squad members join and confirm attendance |
| 05 | **Preference Sync** | Each member selects vibe and budget in about 10 seconds |
| 06 | **AI Recommendation** | AI processes squad inputs and generates top picks |

## 💰 Revenue Model

**Discovery over advertising.** No banner ads, no interruptions. The platform prioritises trust and subtle discovery.

- **Featured Placement:** premium spots in curated shortlists
- **Mood-Based Discovery:** venues appear in vibe-matched recommendations
- **Trend Spotlight:** weekly curated "what's hot nearby"

---

## 🚀 Future Scope

- 🌦️ **Weather-Based Picks:** rainy day suggests cozy indoors, sunny suggests rooftops
- 🎵 **Spotify Mood Sync:** current playlist vibe influences venue recommendations
- 📅 **Calendar Integration:** AI sees free slots and schedules hangouts automatically
- 🔮 **AI Vibe Prediction:** learns squad patterns and pre-suggests before you ask
- 🗺️ **Collaborative Maps:** shared map where squads drop and vote on spots
- 🧠 **Smarter Social AI:** remembers past outings and improves with every plan

---

## ✅ Conclusion

**What Scene?** turns overwhelming social planning into a fast, emotionally engaging experience.

> *It doesn't take the decision. It simplifies it.*
